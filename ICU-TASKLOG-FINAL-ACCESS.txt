============================================================
SCOPE
============================================================
Not a medical device. Rule-based logic only. No AI/ML. No computed score,
ranking or leaderboard. Tier 1–2 tasks are never auto-deferred. The foreground
alarm rings only while the app is open; background notifications/push are out of
scope. Foreground alarm settings and a Test alarm button are on the Me screen.
